What is a Facebook Group?
Answer
A community space on Facebook for people with a shared interest or location. E.g. 'Pune Seniors', 'Green Cooking', 'RWA Block C'. Members post and discuss within the group. Public or Private.

What is Facebook Privacy settings?
Answer
Controls who sees your posts, profile info and photos. Settings → Privacy → choose: Public (anyone), Friends (only your Facebook friends) or Only Me (just you). Review regularly!

What is Facebook Marketplace?
Answer
A free buy/sell platform inside Facebook. List items you want to sell or browse local listings. Like an online local newspaper classifieds section. Meet safely in public to exchange.

What is a YouTube Playlist?
Answer
A saved collection of videos in order. Create your own: tap ⋮ on any video → Save to playlist → New playlist. Or use the 'Watch Later' playlist to save videos to watch anytime.

What is Facebook Messenger?
Answer
Facebook's private messaging app — like WhatsApp but for Facebook friends. Send texts, photos, videos and make free video/audio calls to Facebook friends. Separate app from Facebook.

What is 'Tagging' on Facebook?
Answer
Mentioning someone in a post or photo by typing @their name or tapping their face. They get notified and the post may appear on their profile too. You can untag yourself from unwanted tags!

What is a Facebook Event?
Answer
A way to announce and organise events online. Create an event → invite friends → people RSVP 'Going', 'Maybe' or 'Not Going'. Get reminders automatically. Great for family gatherings!

What does it mean to 'Follow' vs 'Friend' someone on Facebook?
Answer
Friend = mutual — both people see each other's posts. Follow = one-way — you see their public posts but they don't necessarily see yours. You can Follow public figures (politicians, celebrities) without friending.
