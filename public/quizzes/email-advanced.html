How do you spot a phishing email?
Answer
Check: (1) Sender's actual email address (hover/tap — does it look odd?), (2) Urgency ('Act NOW or account will close!'), (3) Grammar mistakes, (4) Links that don't match the company name, (5) Requests for password/OTP/bank details.

What is Two-Factor Authentication for Gmail?
Answer
An extra security step — after entering your password, Google also asks for a code sent to your phone. Even if someone steals your password, they cannot log in without your phone!

How do you block an email sender in Gmail?
Answer
Open an email from them → tap the 3 dots ⋮ → Block '[sender name]'. Future emails from them go straight to Spam. You won't see them unless you check Spam!

What should you check before clicking any link in an email?
Answer
Hover/press on the link to preview the actual URL. Does it match the company? Bank emails link to bank.com, not strange URLs like 'bank-secure-login.xyz'. When in doubt, DON'T click!

What is Gmail's Confidential Mode?
Answer
Compose email → tap ⋮ → Confidential mode → set an expiry time. The email self-destructs after that time and cannot be forwarded, printed or downloaded. Good for sensitive information.

What is 'Safe Browsing' in Gmail?
Answer
Gmail scans all links and attachments for known malware and phishing sites. If a link is dangerous, it shows a RED WARNING before you open it. Always heed these warnings!

How do you recover a hacked Gmail account?
Answer
Go to accounts.google.com/signin/recovery → enter your email → follow recovery steps (backup phone number or recovery email). Then IMMEDIATELY change password and enable Two-Factor Authentication!

What is 'Report Phishing' in Gmail?
Answer
Open the suspicious email → tap ⋮ (3 dots) → Report phishing. This notifies Google who investigates and protects millions of other users from the same attack. A truly helpful action!
