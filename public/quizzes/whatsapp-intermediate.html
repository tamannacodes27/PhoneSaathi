What is WhatsApp Status?
Answer
Short photos, videos or text visible to your contacts for 24 hours, then disappear — like Instagram Stories. Find it in the Status tab. Your contacts can see who viewed their status.

What does 'Disappearing Messages' do?
Answer
Messages in the chat automatically delete after 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days. Set it per chat: open chat → tap name → Disappearing Messages. Great for private conversations.

What is WhatsApp Web?
Answer
Use WhatsApp on your computer! Go to web.whatsapp.com on a browser → tap ⋮ in WhatsApp on your phone → Linked Devices → Link a Device → scan the QR code on screen.

What is the 'Starred Messages' feature?
Answer
Press and hold any message → tap ⭐ Star. Find all starred messages: tap ⋮ → Starred Messages. Great for saving important addresses, phone numbers or information.

What are WhatsApp Communities?
Answer
A larger structure that groups multiple related WhatsApp Groups under one umbrella. Example: a Residents Welfare Association community with sub-groups for Security, Events, and Announcements.

What is 'View Once' media?
Answer
Photos/videos that disappear after the recipient views them once and cannot be forwarded or saved. Tap the 👁️ icon before sending to enable. Good for sharing sensitive documents.

How do you forward a message on WhatsApp?
Answer
Press and hold the message → tap Forward ↪️ → select contacts → Send. WhatsApp labels forwarded messages with a ↪️ icon so recipients know it was forwarded (not original).

What does 'Archive Chat' do on WhatsApp?
Answer
Hides the chat from your main chat list to reduce clutter — it's NOT deleted. Find archived chats by scrolling to the very bottom of your chat list → 'Archived' section.
