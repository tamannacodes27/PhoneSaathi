How do you set WhatsApp privacy (who sees your photo)?
Answer
WhatsApp → Settings → Privacy → Profile Photo → choose 'My Contacts' (only people you know) or 'Nobody' (completely private). Do NOT set to 'Everyone'.

How do you BLOCK someone on WhatsApp?
Answer
Open the chat → tap their name at top → Scroll down → Block. They cannot call you, message you or see your Status. They don't get notified that you blocked them.

What is Two-Step Verification on WhatsApp?
Answer
An extra security layer — a 6-digit PIN you set that is required if WhatsApp is set up on a new phone. Without it, someone who gets your SIM cannot access your WhatsApp. Set it: Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Enable.

How do you back up WhatsApp chats?
Answer
WhatsApp → Settings → Chats → Chat backup → Back Up Now. Link to your Google account to save chats to Google Drive. Do this on Wi-Fi regularly to never lose your messages!

What is WhatsApp's 'Disappearing Messages' feature?
Answer
Messages auto-delete after 24 hours, 7 days or 90 days. Good for privacy in sensitive conversations. Set it per chat: tap contact name → Disappearing messages → choose duration.

How do you report a scam / spam contact on WhatsApp?
Answer
Open the chat → tap the 3 dots ⋮ → Report → then choose Block. This sends info to WhatsApp to investigate and protect others. Always block AND report scammers.

What is a WhatsApp scam 'SIM swap'?
Answer
Fraudsters trick your network operator into giving them a SIM with YOUR number. Then they take over your WhatsApp. Protect yourself: enable Two-Step Verification — without the PIN they cannot access your account!

How do you check which devices are linked to your WhatsApp?
Answer
Settings → Linked Devices. You can see all computers/tablets using your WhatsApp. Tap any device and tap LOG OUT to remove access if you don't recognise it.
