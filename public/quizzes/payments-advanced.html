What is a UPI 'collect request' scam?
Answer
A fraudster sends you a UPI 'collect request' with a message like 'Accept to receive money'. When you enter your PIN, YOU actually PAY THEM. ALWAYS DECLINE unexpected collect requests. You NEVER need a PIN to receive money!

What is a fake customer care number scam?
Answer
Fraudsters put up fake helpline numbers on Google for banks/UPI apps. You call thinking it's the bank, they ask for your UPI PIN/OTP to 'help you'. The real number is ONLY on the back of your debit card or the bank's official website.

What to do if you've been scammed?
Answer
1. Call 1930 (Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately 2. Report in your UPI app → Help → Report fraud 3. Call your bank's helpline (back of your debit card) 4. File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in 5. Visit police station to file FIR. Act FAST — early action improves recovery chances!

What is SIM swap fraud?
Answer
Fraudsters trick your telecom operator into issuing a new SIM with YOUR number, deactivating yours. Then they access your bank OTPs. Red flag: your SIM suddenly stops working. Call your operator IMMEDIATELY if this happens!

What is the RBI Ombudsman?
Answer
A FREE complaint service for banking issues. If your bank doesn't resolve a UPI fraud complaint within 30 days, file with RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in. They investigate and ensure banks compensate victims in eligible cases.

What is 'screen sharing' payment fraud?
Answer
Someone asks you to download AnyDesk, TeamViewer or 'support' apps and share your screen to 'fix a UPI problem'. They then see your OTPs and banking apps live and drain your account. NEVER share your screen for banking help!

What is NACH / Auto-debit?
Answer
National Automated Clearing House — allows companies to automatically deduct from your account on a set date (like insurance premium, loan EMI, subscriptions). You approve it once in your UPI app. Check all active mandates in Google Pay → Autopay.

What is UPI Lite?
Answer
A new feature for small payments (under ₹500) that works WITHOUT internet and doesn't require UPI PIN for each transaction. Load money into UPI Lite wallet once, then tap-to-pay instantly. Great at places with poor signal!
