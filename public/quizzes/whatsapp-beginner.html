What does the ✅ single grey tick on a WhatsApp message mean?
Answer
Your message has been SENT from your phone to WhatsApp's servers. It hasn't reached the recipient's phone yet.

What do ✅✅ double grey ticks mean?
Answer
Your message has been DELIVERED to the recipient's phone. But they haven't opened/read it yet.

What do ✅✅ double BLUE ticks mean?
Answer
The recipient has READ your message. Their WhatsApp was open and they saw it. (They can disable this with Read Receipts setting.)

How do you send a Voice Message on WhatsApp?
Answer
Open the chat → press and HOLD the 🎤 microphone icon → speak your message → release to send. Swipe up while holding to lock the recording so you can speak hands-free!

What is a WhatsApp Group?
Answer
A group chat with multiple people (up to 1024 members). Messages go to everyone in the group at once. Great for family chats, friend groups or community announcements.

How do you share a photo on WhatsApp?
Answer
Open a chat → tap the 📎 paperclip icon → Gallery → select your photo → tap Send. You can select multiple photos at once!

What does 'Mute' a chat do on WhatsApp?
Answer
Stops notification sounds and vibrations for that chat for 8 hours, 1 week or Always. You still receive messages — you just won't hear the ping! Good for busy group chats.

How do you make a WhatsApp Video Call?
Answer
Open the contact's chat → tap the 📹 video camera icon at the top right. Both people need WhatsApp and internet to use this free feature!
