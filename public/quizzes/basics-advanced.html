What is Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Answer
An extra layer of security. Even if someone knows your password, they ALSO need a code sent to your phone. Enable it for Gmail, banking apps and social media for maximum protection.

What is a VPN and when should you use it?
Answer
Virtual Private Network — encrypts your internet connection so others can't spy on your data. Use on public Wi-Fi (cafés, hospitals) to protect banking and personal browsing.

What is Biometric Lock?
Answer
Using your fingerprint or face to unlock the phone. Much harder to bypass than a PIN. Set it up: Settings → Security → Fingerprint / Face Unlock.

What does 'Rooting' or 'Jailbreaking' a phone mean?
Answer
Removing manufacturer restrictions to get full system access. It voids warranty, removes security protections and makes your phone much more vulnerable to malware. NOT recommended.

What is Google Find My Device?
Answer
A free Google feature that lets you locate, lock or remotely ERASE a lost phone. Enable via Settings → Google → Find My Device. Must be signed into Google account.

What is a Factory Reset and when should you use it?
Answer
Wipes ALL data and returns phone to brand-new state. Use when selling/giving away your phone, or if it's badly infected with malware. Always BACKUP first!

What is App Permissions and why does it matter?
Answer
When you install an app, it asks for permissions (camera, microphone, location). Only grant what's needed. A Torchlight app asking for your Contacts is suspicious — deny it!

What is the difference between OS Update and App Update?
Answer
OS Update (e.g. Android 14) = whole phone software, fixes security holes. App Update = updates individual apps. Both are important for security. Enable auto-updates!
