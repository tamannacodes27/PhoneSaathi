What is the Notification Bar / Status Bar?
Answer
The thin strip at the very TOP of your screen. Shows time, battery, Wi-Fi signal, and app alerts. Swipe DOWN to open the Notification Panel.

How do you take a Screenshot?
Answer
Press the Power button + Volume Down button at the same time for 1 second. The screen will flash. The image is saved in your Photos/Gallery.

What is Airplane Mode?
Answer
Turns off ALL wireless signals (calls, mobile data, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth). Use on planes. Your phone still works for photos, games, notes — just no internet or calls.

How do you install a new app?
Answer
Open the Play Store (Google Play) → tap the Search bar → type the app name → tap Install. The app will download and appear on your Home Screen.

What is the difference between Storage and RAM?
Answer
Storage (e.g. 64 GB) = where your photos, apps and files are saved permanently. RAM (e.g. 4 GB) = temporary memory the phone uses while running apps. More RAM = smoother multitasking.

What does 'Clear Cache' do?
Answer
Clears temporary junk files an app has stored. It can fix app slowness and free up space. Your personal data (account, photos) is NOT deleted.

How do you share a photo via WhatsApp?
Answer
Open Gallery → select the photo → tap Share → choose WhatsApp → select the contact → tap Send. The photo will arrive instantly!

What is 'Do Not Disturb' mode?
Answer
Silences all calls, notifications and sounds. Find it in Quick Settings (swipe down). Useful during sleep or meetings. You can allow certain contacts to still ring through.
