What is Facebook?
Answer
The world's largest social network. Share life updates, photos and videos. Connect with family, friends and local communities. Available free on Android and iPhone.

What is a Facebook Profile?
Answer
Your personal page on Facebook. It shows your name, profile photo, cover photo and posts you've shared. Only YOU can post on your own Profile (unless others tag you).

What does 'Like' (👍) mean on Facebook?
Answer
Tapping 👍 on a post tells the person you appreciated their post. It takes one tap and is completely free. You can also react with ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 😡 — press and hold 👍 to see options!

What is a Facebook Page?
Answer
Like a Profile but for businesses, celebrities or organisations. You can 'Like' or 'Follow' a Page to get their updates. E.g. your local hospital may have a Facebook Page.

What is YouTube?
Answer
The world's largest free video website by Google. Watch videos on any topic — news, cooking, health, music, devotional, comedy. Create a free account to subscribe to channels!

What does 'Subscribe' mean on YouTube?
Answer
Clicking Subscribe on a YouTube channel means their new videos appear in your feed and you get notified. Free to subscribe to any channel! Tap Subscribe → Bell 🔔 for notifications.

What is a Facebook News Feed?
Answer
The scrolling main page of Facebook showing posts from your friends and Pages you follow. Like a personalised newspaper updated constantly with new posts from people you know!

What does 'Share' mean on Facebook?
Answer
Reposting someone else's post to your own Profile or Story so YOUR friends can also see it. Shows 'Shared from [original poster]'. A way to spread interesting content!
