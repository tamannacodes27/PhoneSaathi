Help Centre
How can we help you? 🤝
You'll find answers to common questions, helpful tips, and ways to reach us below
Quick tips
🐢
Go at your own pace
There is no rush. You can pause a lesson any time and come back to it later — your progress is always saved.
🔁
Repeat as many times as you like
You can redo any lesson or quiz as many times as you want. Practice makes it easier!
📶
Need a good internet connection
Make sure your phone or device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data before you start a lesson.
🔤
Make text bigger
Go to My Profile and turn on Larger text if the words feel too small to read comfortably.
🔐
Forgot your password?
On the sign-in page, tap Forgot password? and we will send a reset link to your email address.
👤
Update your profile anytime
Your name, skill level, and preferences can all be changed from the My Profile page at any time.
